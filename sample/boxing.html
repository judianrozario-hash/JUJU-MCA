Welcome to Knockout Boxing Academy

Welcome to Knockout Boxing Academy, where people of all ages are welcome to learn boxing. Our academy provides professional boxing practice for students who want to participate in competitions, as well as training for people who simply want to improve their fitness and confidence.

Whether your goal is to become a professional boxer or simply stay healthy, our experienced trainers will help you improve your strength, speed, stamina, technique and discipline.

Our Training Programs

Beginner Boxing

Learn basic boxing techniques, footwork, punching, defense and proper training methods.

Competition Training

Advanced training for students who want to participate in amateur and professional boxing competitions.

Fitness Boxing

Boxing-based workouts designed to improve fitness, strength, stamina and overall health.
